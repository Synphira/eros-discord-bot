# The Tower

Thirty-five floors of lust, monsters, and domain lords. A boss every five floors; **Eros** at the top.

## What you’ll find

| Kind of room | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Hallways | Monsters, traps, chests, events, stairs |
| Domain chambers | Clergy bosses on floors 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 |
| Throne | Floor 35 — Eros |
| Relics | From cycle 2: sometimes an empty chamber holds a relic boss instead |
