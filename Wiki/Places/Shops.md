# Shops & hubs

Open `/shop` for the hub, or jump straight to a counter with the commands below.

## Syn & Berry’s

| Counter | Who | Command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gear & potions | Berry | `/shop`, `/buy`, `/sell` |
| Quest board | Berry | `/quests` |
| Parlour | Syn | `/parlour` |
| Curse ledger | Syn | `/curses` |

You can **Talk** with Syn and Berry from the shop and quest panels (General, Clergy, Residents, Temple — clergy Talk is mainly from boss victory).

## Covar’s Cursed Shop

**Covar Zephyren** sells and reclaims **living gear**. Torn pieces come back for free. You can Talk with him — or take him to bed.

`/cursedshop`

## Residents

`/residents` — settled folk with their own services and errands:

| Resident | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Madame Vex** | Living-clothing fittings and seasonal outfits |
| **Pip** | Traps (self-appointed trapwright) |
| **Sister Nectar** | The Milking Shrine |
| **The Pale Cartographer** | Reading the next few rooms |
