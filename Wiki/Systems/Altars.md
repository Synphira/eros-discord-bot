# Altars

Rooms where you can make an offering and take the tower’s notice — blessings, risks, and seasonal variants (pumpkin altars during All Hallows’ Heat, and so on).

You’ll know one when `/explore` drops you in front of it. Effects are shown on the panel before you commit.
