# Content options

`/options` · `e,fetish_options` · `e,sluttify [on/off]`

The game is **always adult**. These toggles choose **which** themes show up — not whether sex exists.

| Group | Default | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | On | 13 |
| **Body** | Opt-in | 15 |
| **Kink** | Opt-in | 17 |
| **Fetish** | Opt-in | 25 |

Use the menu, or toggle one theme by name/key with `e,fetish_options`. `e,sluttify on` turns every theme on at once (prefix only).
