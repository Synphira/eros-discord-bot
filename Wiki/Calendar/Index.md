# Holiday seasons

The tower keeps a calendar. When a season is active, hallway pests wear holiday names, lust and events lean harder, Syn and the shop folk dress up, Vex stocks souvenir outfits, and `/status` may offer seasonal treats. Souvenirs and titles you earn usually **stay** after the window closes.

Only one season runs at a time.

## The year

| Season | When (roughly) |
| --- | --- |
| **Heartstring Claim** | Mid-February |
| **Carnival of Beads** | Around Fat Tuesday |
| **Hare's Hunt** | Easter weekend |
| **Fool's Trap** | April 1 |
| **Maypole Raising** | Early May |
| **Longest Day** | June solstice |
| **Pride Parade** | June Pride window |
| **Pip's Slow Spell** | US Labor Day (first Monday in September) |
| **All Hallows' Heat** | Late October into November 1 |
| **Feast of the Willing** | US Thanksgiving week |
| **Yule of Surrender** | Late December |
| **Midnight Oath** | New Year |

Exact start and end times are on the bot when a season is live (and in `/status` / Syn Talk while it lasts).

## What changes during a season

- Stronger lust multipliers and/or bonus LP for submitting to favoured types
- More events (and sometimes more elites)
- Every hallway monster gets a seasonal alias
- Named folk change clothes (Syn, Berry, Covar, Vex, Sister Nectar, the Pale Cartographer, and others)
- Vex’s seasonal rack and keep-forever souvenirs
- Treats you can spend from `/status`
- Seasonal titles
- Extra Talk topics with Syn

Some seasons also suggest related content options (for example Halloween scares, Pride parade themes, Maypole binding). You still choose those in `/options`.

**All Hallows' Heat** is the only season that keeps “Heat” in the name.
