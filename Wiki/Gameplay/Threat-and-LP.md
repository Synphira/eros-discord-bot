# Threat & Lust Points

## Lust Points (LP)

Earn LP from combat, quests, clears, events, altars, and more. Spend it on:

- Levels and stats (`/levelup`)
- Berry’s shop
- Removing or suppressing curses
- Syn’s parlour marks
- Hybrid / chimera work
- Tearing off living gear (and related costs)

### Support bonuses

Open `/status` → **Bonuses** (this support server):

| Perk | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Wear the server **tag** | +5% LP · title *Tagged Devotee* |
| **Boost** the server | +5% LP · 10% off LP prices · extra Lich's Phylactery charge per run · titles *Tower Patron* / *Beloved of the Tower* |

Drop the tag or boost and those titles go away. Bonuses work in DMs and other servers.

## Threat

Threat rises mainly with how much **LP** you’ve banked and how many **curses** you carry (appearance from gear can nudge it too). Higher Threat means stronger, hornier monsters and more monster-heavy rooms.

Getting rich makes the tower hungrier. Manage curses if you want the halls quieter.

Monster **HP** also scales a little with your **level** and a lot with your **cycle** — deep climbs stay multi-turn even when your STR is absurd. See [Combat](Combat.md).
