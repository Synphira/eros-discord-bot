# Defeat, cycles & burdens

## Defeat

When **Defiance** hits 0:

- You’re sent back to **floor 1** at **level 1**
- You take a new **curse**
- Ordinary gear is usually lost
- You **keep** LP, curses, living gear, marks, titles, achievements, hybrids, and long progress

## Cycles

Clear **floor 35 (Eros)** and you start a new **cycle**:

- Same delver, gear, titles, and progress
- A clear bonus in LP
- Monsters and bosses hit harder and pay better (about **×1.3** per cycle)
- Curses brand more readily
- Relic bosses can appear from cycle 2

Climb, clear, climb hungrier — that’s the long game.

## Burdens

At the start of a cycle on **floor 1**, open `/status` and take or shed up to **3 burdens** (**+25% LP** each). Once you leave floor 1, those three stay locked until you clear the tower.

The five torments:

- **Hungry Tower**
- **Glass Defiance**
- **Slow Feet**
- **Tempting Dark**
- **Branded Start**

Exact effects are written on the status panel.

### Oaths and rerolls

- **Oaths** — up to **2** extra torments mid-climb (**+10% LP** each)
- **Rerolls** — pay to shuffle into a different torment (never an oath). Price climbs as you go deeper and resets when you’re back on floor 1
