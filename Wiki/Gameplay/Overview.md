# How to play

## Create your delver

1. Pick a **body type**.
2. Choose a **submission stance** (Eager → Resistant). This attitude lasts — defeat does not wipe it.
3. Name yourself.

`/restart` starts a new body and stance while keeping titles, achievements, and most long progress (story quests reset).

## Explore

`/explore` rolls the next room. You might find:

- A monster (or elite)
- A trap
- A treasure chest
- An event
- Stairs to the next floor
- An altar
- A strange biome room

In a fight, use **Fight**, **Flee**, or **Submit** (buttons or commands).

## What to watch

| Resource | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Defiance** | Your will. Hits 0 → defeat |
| **Lust** | Builds in combat; orgasm at 100 drains Defiance |
| **LP** | Spendable currency — also feeds Threat |
| **Curses** | Stick around and raise Threat |
| **Threat** | Higher = tougher, lustier tower |

## Between rooms

- `/rest` — clear lust and fully restore Defiance (5-minute cooldown)
- `/levelup` — spend LP on STR, AGI, RES, or a whole level
- `/shop` — Berry’s gear and potions, Covar’s living gear, Syn’s parlour, Berry’s quests
- `/status` — sheet, Threat, burdens, support bonuses, seasonal treats
- `/options` — content themes

## What survives a break

Titles, achievements, marks, living-gear unlocks, hybrids, admirers, codex fragments, and your records. Normal shop gear and boss trophies are usually gone when you break.
