# Combat

When a hallway pest (or elite, or boss) stops you, pick an action.

## Actions

| Action | What you’re doing |
| --- | --- |
| **Fight** | Hurt them. Wins pay LP (and can still earn curses) |
| **Flee** | Agility check — escape, or get caught worse |
| **Submit** | Lean in. LP and lust rise; you can satisfy them for a soft clear |

Buttons do the same thing as the commands.

## Stats that matter

| Stat | In a fight |
| --- | --- |
| **STR** | Fight damage (diminishing returns at very high STR — more STR still helps, it just won’t one-shot the tower forever) |
| **AGI** | Dodge chance (caps at 50%); flee chance (caps around AGI 10). Past that, AGI is mostly dodge |
| **RES** | Softens lust hits (never below a floor of the monster’s raw lust) |
| **Submission** | Tradeoff — see below |

## Submission stance

Chosen at create (and raised/lowered by some gear and curses). It is **not** a dump stat.

| Lean | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Resistant** (low) | Lust hits land **softer**. Satisfy chance is worse. Fight is the comfortable path |
| **Neutral** | Baseline |
| **Eager** (high) | Lust hits land **harder**. Satisfy is easier. Successful **Submit** actions pay **bonus LP** |

You’ll see a one-line reminder at the start of a fight when your stance isn’t neutral.

## Lust and Defiance

- Hits, teasing, and submission raise **lust** (scaled by RES and Submission).
- At **100 lust**, you climax and lose **Defiance**.
- **0 Defiance** means you’re broken — see [Defeat](Defeat-Cycles-Burdens.md).

Curses can come from climax, satisfying a foe, winning, or defeat — not only from losing.

## Bosses

A domain lord waits every **5 floors**. **Eros** waits on **35**. Clear them by defeating **or** satisfying them. After a clear you can **Talk** with that lord on the victory screen.

From **cycle 2** onward, an empty lord’s chamber may hold a **relic boss** instead — a hungry sacred object, not one of the six domain lords. At most two relics appear per cycle.

Holiday seasons can rename hallway pests, raise lust pressure, boost submit LP, and lean events or elites harder.
