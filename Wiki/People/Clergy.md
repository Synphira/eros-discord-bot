# Domain lords

Six lords hold the gated floors. Clear them by defeating **or** satisfying them. A **trophy** drops on clear (lost if you later break). Victory **Talk** unlocks after a clear.

| Floor | Name |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | **Mimic Broodmother** |
| 10 | **Succubus Queen** |
| 15 | **Incubus King** |
| 20 | **Verdant Dryad** |
| 25 | **Lich Lord** |
| 30 | **Alpha Beast** |

**Syn** is tied to the same order as these lords, but she runs the parlour and curse ledger rather than a numbered gate.

Ask about them in Talk once you’ve earned the topics — Syn, Berry, and residents have opinions.
