# Syn & Berry

## Syn

Runs the **parlour** and the **curse ledger**. Marks (tattoos and piercings), Suppress, and Remove all go through her. She Talks — carefully — and dresses for the holidays when a season is on.

| Command | What she does |
| --- | --- |
| `/parlour` | Permanent marks for LP |
| `/curses` | Page, suppress, or remove curses |

## Berry

Syn’s wife. Runs the **shop** and **quest board** — sunny, mercantile, and devout. Buy gear and potions, sell ordinary items, and take her errands for bonus LP.

| Command | What she does |
| --- | --- |
| `/shop` | Gear and potions |
| `/quests` | Errands for LP |

Talk to either of them from the shop or quest panels.
