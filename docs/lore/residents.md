## The Residents

The tower's settled souls — not clergy, not bosses — who keep trades and stories of their own. Full art briefs live in `art_reference.md`.

### How someone settles

**Resident** is not a formal rank. It is a **soft status**: clergy tolerate them and trade with them. Nobody stamps papers. People **squat**, and the **land keeps them**.

Common pattern (both, not either/or): the tower **remade** them into someone who fits (Covar's incubus hybrid; Nectar's milky faith), *and/or* they **fell in love with a system** (living gear, traps, milk, maps) hard enough that leaving stopped making sense. Jobs follow desire — Listener, Trapwright, shrine-keeper, Cartographer — more than recruitment.

**Hard line vs clergy:** no domain, no priesthood, no floor claim. Soft exceptions: **Nectar** feels clerical-adjacent; **Covar** is almost clergy by Syn's affection alone.

**Exile** (Vex cast from Covar's back room) proves residents can be thrown out by whoever's space they use — settled is not sacred.

**Player delvers becoming residents** is a soft ending fantasy for later — not in scope to build now.
