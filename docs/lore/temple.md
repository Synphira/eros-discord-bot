# Temple Systems — Design, Rules, Theology

Status: living design document. Split from the former monolithic `docs/lore.md` on 2026-10-23. See `README.md` for the full index.

## What the Temple's Design Means

The temple was not built merely to be explored. It was built to change the people who enter it.

Syn's particular act of faith was helping to create a place that draws visitors deeper into desire. The traps, monsters, hybrid forms, altars, curses, and other systems can therefore be understood as parts of the temple's ministry, even when the player does not yet know that.

The bosses are not simply obstacles placed before Eros. Each one helped shape the temple and continues to serve her in their own domain. Their floors, traps, monsters, and preferred forms of temptation should reflect their personalities and contributions.

Known contributions include:

- **Syn and the Succubus Queen** created the temple's traps together.
- **The Verdant Dryad** contributed the ideas behind the tentacle and vine traps.
- **Syn and the Incubus King** created the spell that helps spawn monsters throughout the temple.
- **The Lich Lord** taught Syn how to use lust energy.
- **Syn** designed the Alpha Beast's floors with plenty of room for his pack to roam.

## The Dungeon's Rules

What the game's mechanics mean inside the world:

- **Defeat is submission.** A delver is not killed by the dungeon; they are overwhelmed and give in to carnal desire and depravity. Being "broken" means their restraint lost, not their life.
- **Resetting is not lore.** Returning after defeat, starting over, and other run resets are pure game mechanics. They have no in-world explanation, and no character should ever acknowledge them.
- **Cycles are Eros's own creation.** Unlike the temple, which her clergy built without her direction, the cycles are hers: she made them out of desire, to keep delvers trapped in the dungeon, going around again and again until they give in to depravity and lust. There is no finishing the dungeon and leaving unchanged; there is only surrender, sooner or later.
- **Curses are the marks of the dungeon's lust magic.** They are the dungeon's magic altering a delver — brands left on a body it has taken an interest in. No designer authored them; they **grew from the land**. Clergy named and study what the temple was already doing. Soft yes, they **want** something: each pulls you nearer its monster type. Removing one (LP rites or an Eros visit) is **scrubbing the tower's fingerprint** off. In-world they wear both **clergy formal names** (Wild, Infernal, Viscous, Necrotic, Botanical) and **delver slang**. Mid-fight, a curse is the **domain stamping you through the monster**, and your body answering that interest with a brand. A body loud with many curses is Attention candy to Eros, a favourite unstable essay to the Lich, something Berry frets over and Syn sketches. Mimic curses are not special doctrine — same rules as any type.
- **Transformations are the delver's own lust taken form.** A hybrid shape is not imposed from outside; it is what a delver's desire looks like when the dungeon lets it become visible and wearable. Same authorship as curses: **land + desire**, not a Syn blueprint (though she sketches every new shape fondly).
- **Curse vs hybrid (doctrine).** A curse is the **tower's interest** stamped on you. A hybrid is ***your* lust wearing you**. Same lust magic, different sentence: one is "we noticed you," the other is "this is what you wanted made flesh."
- **Hybrids don't erase.** Once a form unlocks, you can **shelve** it — you cannot truly erase it. It waits in the skin.
- **Chimera** (fused forms) is **pack/brood logic**: many appetites, one body. Desire refusing a single shape, written the way the Broodmother keeps and the Alpha claims — plurality as belonging.
- **Clergy read them as published work.** Syn sketches; domain lords of matching type get proprietary interest; Berry worries about permanence; Covar loves body-change the way he loves gear-change.
- **Lust Points are lust energy.** Lust energy is the raw power of the dungeon and the source of all magic within it — the same force the clergy built with, the monsters run on, and delvers carry out of fights as spendable energy. Lust Points are that energy, measured.

## Floor 35 — Throne and Heart

Floors 31–34 are her **dreaming court**: the stretch where the reshaping land moves fastest, half inside her dreams, so rooms arrive half-decided and manifestations are common — altars at every turn, tentacles in the candlelight, a voice that already knows your name. It is less stocked than the domains below and more personal. The danger is not the monsters. It is being individually noticed for four floors.

Floor 35 is the **throne and heart of the temple**: her throne set over the source of the dungeon's lust magic, the well the whole building drinks from. The clergy raised the throne room around that source the way a chapel is raised around a relic. The ruined altar young Syn knelt at **rests before the throne**, carried up and set there — what she found at the bottom of her life, enthroned at the top of her work.

There is no exit at the top. Win against Eros or surrender to her, floor 35 ends the same way: **she returns you herself**, pleased, and the cycle takes you around again. The last thing a delver learns in a run is that reaching the goddess was never the way out. It is the reason there is no way out.

## Altars & Eros's Attention

The altars were built by the clergy, as offering points raised throughout their temple — and Eros genuinely uses them. Of everything the clergy built on interpretation alone, the altars are where their faith turned out to be simply correct: kneel at one, and she can see you.

**Floor altars** are clergy mirrors — pipes they built. The **First Altar** before the throne is an **older channel**; same goddess, different depth. All of them reach her eye.

**Pray / tithe / offer:** to Eros the flavours blur; to the fairies they don't. **Tithe** reads Berry-practical faith; **offer yourself** reads Syn-flavoured. They rib each other for which they reach for. She is hard to offend at an altar — she takes what she's given.

**Attention is her literal regard.** Devotion draws her eye: satisfying the tower's creatures, kneeling at her altars, wearing Syn's marks, making offerings. The more a delver lives as an act of worship — whether they know it or not — the more distinctly she notices them among all the delvers in her halls. When her regard grows full she answers — but only ever in part — and afterward she **looks away a little**: the visit spends the fullness down. Clarity, not a coin purse, but the meter still drops when she's done gifting.

A visit is a **partial manifestation**: a white tentacle curling out of the dark, a voice from everywhere, warmth and red eyes in candlelight. She never appears in full below floor 35. Gift choice is **whim — weather**; she is keeping count of return visits with **possessive bookkeeping and amused fondness**.

What the gifts mean:

- **Lust-light (LP)** — literal lust energy from her well, pressed into the delver's hands.
- **Eros's Favour** — lubricant for surrender: she makes giving in easier and sweeter for a few floors.
- **Curse purge** — she plucks a brand that bored her (the temple's fingerprint removed on a whim).
- **Defiance heal** — restoring the toy so it can break again.

Whether altar response **validates** the clergy's interpretation or she simply enjoys worship stays **deliberately open forever**.

## Monsters, Traps & Living Gear

**Monsters are shaped, not born.** The monster-spawning working that Syn and the Incubus King created together draws in raw appetite and shapes it out of lust energy: things with teeth, hunger, and a use for delvers, set loose where delvers walk. That is why the halls never empty and why the stock of a domain always suits its lord — the spell shapes what the land, and its ruler, call for.

Their minds vary. Spawned stock runs mostly on appetite, with flashes of cunning where a domain's idiom rewards it. Above them stand genuinely sapient beings — bosses, and residents who are people rather than stock — who scheme, bargain, keep shops, fall in love, and hold grudges. The line between "monster" and "resident" is sapience, not shape.

**Edge cases:**

- **Admirers** — A briefly sapient crush *and* a favour the domain lends you. Real enough to follow and leave gifts; when they wander off they simply **forgot you** — appetite moved on. Syn flirts with the idea some monsters keep favourites; Berry treats admirers as creepy pets. Both tones belong in Ask-abouts.
- **Elites** — Same stock under louder lust-magic weather; the domain showing off for Eros. Not people — just louder sentences.
- **Named fight forms** (Desperate HuCow Mira, Living Doll Lillia, etc.) — hybrid of **person + domain pressure**: the NPC is present, forced into a fight shape by crisis and the land.
- **Stock → resident** — rare, only if a lord or Syn notices long enough. Don't lean on it; the door exists.

**Traps are part of the land.** Syn and the Succubus Queen designed them — with the Verdant Dryad inspiring the tentacle and vine work — but nobody rewinds them by hand. Traps were built into the domains themselves, and the reshaping land re-dreams them along with the rooms: sprung plates reset, vines regrow hungry, and a corridor that caught you yesterday has thought of something new. Syn's philosophy, built into every mechanism: a trap should leave you changed, not just caught.

**Event bosslets** (Confessor, False Exit, Tea Ceremony, Mirror Duet, Gorgon Matron, Spring Keeper, and kin) are mostly **land improvisations** — lust magic making one-off people for a room. They are not relics and not clergy.

- **Confessor** — Sibling hunger to the Empty Confessional, given legs when you leave unshriven. When the booth voice is Syn's, that is Syn remote-flirting; the fight form is still the land answering.
- **False Exit / Door Mimic** — Queen trap philosophy with a hinge (mimic gag, her craft).
- **Aphrodisiac Tea Ceremony** — Dryad hospitality rite gone mobile, with Berry-adjacent manners (she buys the leaves).
- **Mirror Duet** — Reflection fight / vanity twin energy; Queen-adjacent craft, land improvising a dance partner.
- **Gorgon Matron / Spring Keeper** — Rare sapient stock, almost residents — dignified enough to negotiate, not given floors.

### Strange rooms — Ask-about beats

**Menu:** one omnibus topic — **“strange rooms.”** Lines rotate by which events the delver has met (not a dump of all six).

**Unlock:** met that event once → **short** line; met 3+ times *or* survived/fought the bosslet → **deep** variant (same angle, more bite).

**Syn overall:** proud tinkerer — land copying “us” — plus **flirty** specifically about the Confessor (her remote voice); professional about the rest.

**Berry overall:** soft on Tea (she buys the leaves); **sharp** on Confessor and False Exit; **worried-mom** on Gorgon Matron and Spring Keeper (almost people).

| Room | Syn (short) | Berry (short) |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| **Confessor** | *“If the booth sounded like me, that was me saying hi. The legs are the land’s idea.”* | *“Leaving unshriven is how you get chased. Sit next time.”* |
| **False Exit** | *“Queen’s hinge philosophy. If the door knocks back, believe it.”* | *“Too-convenient doors waste your afternoon. And sometimes your clothes.”* |
| **Tea Ceremony** | *“Dryad hospitality went mobile. Drink if you mean it.”* | *“I buy those leaves. Be polite to the cup.”* |
| **Mirror Duet** | *“Vanity with choreography. Don’t compliment it first.”* | *“If it looks like you and flirts better, walk away smiling.”* |
| **Gorgon Matron** | *“Almost a resident. Negotiate. Don’t be rude to stone.”* | *“She’s a person enough to worry me. Don’t pick a fight for fun.”* |
| **Spring Keeper** | *“Same — rare sapient stock. The spring has manners; use yours.”* | *“Almost family, no floor. Be gentle. Please.”* |

Deep variants keep the same angle (Syn’s Confessor flirt thickens; Berry’s Tea warmth / Confessor scold / almost-people worry deepen) — writers expand at implement time; do not contradict the shorts.

**Living gear took root.** Syn made the first cursed and living pieces by hand — collars, garments, and toys with appetites of their own. Then her designs **took root** in the dungeon's lust magic the way seeds copy a pattern: the place began producing living gear on its own in chests, mimic broods, and dark corners where desire pools.

**Minds.** Only **Syn-made** pieces have real minds. Dungeon-grown stock is **feral** — appetite without the craftsperson's spark. The Original (Madame Vex's dress, later Covar's) sits at the top of that scale: first, most personlike, Syn's masterpiece. It bonds one wearer at a time. After the **Restore the Dress** quest, **Covar wears it** at the counter — a new chapter, not a lesser one; the dress is alive on him, not on a stand.

**Desire.** Gear wants to be **worn and used** — intimacy is its purpose. It remembers who wore it and resents removal. From the gear's view (and Covar's framing): negotiating a piece off intact is a **breakup**; tearing it free is **violence**. Temperaments can be refit (Vex's work); the want underneath stays.

**Covar** collects, catalogues, and rehomes strays — matchmaker more than merchant. His goal is all of it at once: pair gear with delvers who will keep them happy; breed accessories to match Syn's masterworks (the old tiara plans and their cousins); shepherd a living wardrobe ecosystem. His own living accessories are spoiled pets that obey because he spoils them.

**The fairies.** Berry wears **none** — the sensation on her skin creeps her out. She both **demands Syn stop making** living jewellery and is quietly glad when pieces leave ("fewer creepy things on my wife"). Syn focuses on **ink** now, but still occasionally fiddles with living jewellery — true **Syn-made minds**. The rings wander because she **underfeeds them attention** and they sulk-walk; they usually turn up on **Covar's counter**, where he sighs and shelves them. Soft cameo now; a **"return Syn's rings"** errand is fine later.

## Theology & the Cycles

### What Eros Is

Eros is the **eldritch Goddess of Depravity** — not a polite love-goddess in a nicer dress, but something older and hungrier, typed Eldritch in the tower's own bestiary for a reason. She was once worshipped more widely; by the time young Syn found the ruined altar, the world had nearly finished forgetting her. A goddess of her kind lives on faith and lust fed to her: without worshippers, she thins. Without Syn, she would have gone out.

Through Syn and the temple, **faith in her is being restored**, and with it her power. Every delver who descends, every surrender, every orgasm offered inside those walls is not only ministry — it is fuel. The more lust the temple gathers, the more of herself she can hold.

Her longer ambition is larger than the throne room. Should she gain enough power from the lust of those inside the temple, she seeks to **manifest physically outside it** — a full body in the world beyond the stone — and to **affect that world with her power**. What that looks like is a **soft apocalypse**: lust weather, cults blooming, cities going strange — not a neat liberation and not a clean catastrophe. Designers (and the goddess) do not pick a single moral. Floor 35 is where she is most present now; it is not where she intends to stay forever. The temple is a battery and a body-in-progress. The cycles keep the battery filling. **How close** she is stays fuzzy — never pin a year.

### Doctrine

Eros's doctrine can be said in one line: **surrender is the offering.** What she ultimately wants from delvers is not service, conversion, or tribute — it is the moment, and the life-long habit, of giving in to lust and depravity. What a surrendered delver becomes afterwards — worshipper, resident, pet, or simply someone who goes around again more honestly — is secondary. Depravity is not the road to her gift; it is the gift's destination — and, for her, the meal that rebuilds a goddess.

The **cycles are her answer to the temple.** The clergy built for decades on dreams and signs, never directed and never confirmed. When the work stood finished, Eros responded — her first and only unambiguous act upon the place. She laid the cycles over her temple: delvers who reach the top are not released, only returned, to go around again and again until they give in. Their gift to her was a temple; her gift to them was a trap with no exit but surrender — and a pump that never stops feeding her.

The clergy know the cycles are hers, not theirs. They recognise her hand in them, and count them as their one proof that she answered — the founding generation's interpretation, made true exactly once. Everything else they built remains interpretation; the cycles are response.

Who understands that the cycles are also a **launch** — battery enough for her to walk the world again:

- **Syn** knows, and she is a **proud accomplice**. She wants her goddess in the world.
- **The Lich Lord** knows; he has modelled it. Data first, horror second, pride somewhere in the footnotes.
- **Everyone else** does not know, or only half-guesses. Many still think they built her a home, not a rocket.
- **Berry** knows because Syn told her. She still feeds Syn through it — marriage holding the leash while ministry aims at the horizon.
- **The Drowned Saint** would **hitch a ride** if the seal upstairs ever breaks; she does not get a vote, but she has a plan.

What Eros thinks of her people, personally:

- **Syn** — Possessive. *Her* favourite little fairy slut: the one who found the altar when faith was nearly gone, and the one Eros will not share the credit for saving her with anyone else.
- **Mimic Broodmother** — Amused. Furniture with opinions; a useful mouth she does not take too seriously.
- **Succubus Queen** — A tool with good taste. Vanity ministry that dresses worship nicely.
- **Incubus King** — Embarrassing. She tolerates the feed; she does not respect the performance.
- **Verdant Dryad** — Background comfort. Not closely watched; hospitality is pleasant scenery, not a favourite.
- **Lich Lord** — Amused that he will not admit he serves her. She knows he has modelled the launch anyway.
- **Alpha Beast** — Good-dog energy, even from a goddess. Honest worship without schemes earns soft regard.
- **Berry** — Fond of the one who feeds her favourite. Lay devotion with a kitchen; not clergy, still noticed.

That she needed Syn, and that she is using the temple to climb back into the world, stands.
