# Wandering NPCs

Status: living design document. Split from the former monolithic `docs/lore.md` on 2026-10-23. See `README.md` for the full index.

Story NPCs who appear on floors (see `Engine::NPCSystem`). Art briefs in `art_reference.md`.

### After lives (bible tone)

Overall: **cozy corruption** — happy endings that are also traps — with per-character colour (Mira/Tobin/Lacta warm; Lillia/Elara cold objecthood; Marcus pathetic; Thomas sighing comedy). After their floor stories, delvers can meet them again as **soft cameos at their new homes** (Nectar’s shrine, Syn’s parlour, Covar’s, nests, apiary).

### Mira — the Milk-Swollen Delver

Competent mid-rank delver hit by a bad lactation curse. Soft curvy, medium-tall, brown hair, freckles. Voice is surprisingly normal until the milk talk starts; ashamed but addicted — begs for help and for more. Knows **Sister Nectar** and ends at her shrine. Has been to Syn's parlour: **Syn really wants to pierce her nipples**; Mira desperately resists temptation and keeps saying no. Berry knows her from those visits too.

**After:** Willing shrine fixture — milking as worship — and half-staff between sessions, helping **Sister Lacta**. Still says no to the piercings. Cameo at Nectar’s.

### Lillia — the Reshaped Adventurer

Serious fighter; living armour was a loot mistake. Always a little dumb; the armour makes it worse. By the end she is a **blank doll** with no self left — Syn/Berry-scale exaggeration when "done." Ends as a **Syn parlour regular**. The **Mimic Broodmother** claims her as brood-adjacent.

**After:** Living décor Syn shows off; no real conversation left — at most a **doll giggle** if poked. Cameo as object/flavour, not a person.

### Sister Elara — the Latex Priestess

**Sister Nectar's peer and rival** (holy milking vs latex). Modest habit, soft midsize, dark hair under veil; fights the latex to the last prayer. After the change, her voice is a soft echo of old prayers, corrupted. Ends as **Syn's material sample / parlour curiosity**. The **Lich Lord** finds her interesting data.

**After:** Same object register as Lillia — sample on display; poke gets one **corrupted prayer-echo**, not dialogue. Cameo as material, not peer.

### Marcus — the Succubus Chaser

Read too many dirty pamphlets; zero field experience. Scrawny, charms, potions, obvious bulge; whiny frustrated voice. Useful idiot who sometimes helps. Ends as **someone's pet**. **Madame Vex** knew worse in the Queen's court.

**After:** Pet of a **random floor succubus who got lucky** — not court prestige, just pathetic luck. Cameo on demon floors if that succubus is around; Vex still rolls her eyes.

### Thomas — the Over-Geared Delver

Whale spender who min-maxed into poverty. Average soft man under the gear; panic plus arousal he won't admit; horny whimper under the panic. Ends as **Covar's cautionary employee**. Covar knows him by name and sighs; at least one piece on him is **Syn-made**; **Pip** once tried to "upgrade" a strap.

**After:** Still on Covar’s floor — living warning label / underpaid help. Soft cameo at the living-gear shop.

### Alex — the Lost Newbie

They/them; genitals ambiguous. Pilgrim who thought the ruins were ordinary. Slow sweet corruption: androgynous starter gear, blushing mess; nervous stammer → dreamy moan. **Berry tried to send them home once.**

**After:** Stayed — **nest regular**. Berry’s rescue failed gently; she doesn’t pretend otherwise. Cameo in nest/trap spaces, dreamy and settled.

### Tobin — the Beekeeper

Mortal who wandered in; the bees kept him. Broad, calm, mild, slightly uncanny kindness; veil pushed up like a hat; unhurried voice. Wants **Syn and the Dryad's blessing** on the apiary. The **Verdant Dryad** knows him and shares flowers.

**After:** Warm fixture of the apiary; blessing may or may not arrive, but he keeps tending. Soft cameo on Dryad-adjacent floors.

### Sister Lacta — Milking Station Technician

**Sister Nectar's lay sister / ordained assistant.** True name **Sister Lacta** (holy dairy naming, same pattern as Nectar). In play the station can stay “the Technician”; **Nectar uses her name**. Cheerful clinical connoisseur in a lab coat; bright customer-service filthy. Reports to and reveres Nectar.

**After:** Already home — station life *is* the after. Works alongside Mira when she’s between offerings.

### Trapped Delver variants

Forever **generic** faces (stocks, glory hole, mirror gazer, mimic chest) — one-shot flavour, half-begging and half-enjoying. The **Succubus Queen** designed them; **Pip** maintains them. Art briefs stay as written. No personal afterlives.
