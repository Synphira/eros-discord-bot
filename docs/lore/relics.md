# Relic Bosses

Status: living design document. Split from the former monolithic `docs/lore.md` on 2026-10-23. See `README.md` for the full index.

When a domain lord is away — conclave, rites at the top of the tower — the threshold is sometimes held by a **relic**: an object fed offerings long enough to wake. Most are leftover worship-things; a few are failed gifts or vessels. The **First Altar Stone** is special (see below). At most two relic thrones appear per cycle. Art briefs in `art_reference.md`.

### Relic gossip (who knows what)

- **Pale Bride** — Clergy gossip; the Queen finds it hilarious. **Syn denies it forever** anyway.
- **Mirror Queen** — The Succubus Queen pretends the mirror has “always been there.”
- **Empty Confessional** — Only **Syn and the Lich** know it was her gift gone wrong; both refuse to discuss it.
- **Chalice** — The Dryad talks about it warmly if asked; Syn and the Queen toast with the story at drinks.
- **First Altar Stone** — Syn’s embarrassment is public among clergy; Berry knows she’s flustered and finds it cute.
- **Hoardheart** — Alpha’s “weird big dog” read is open chatter.
- **Drowned Saint** — Most clergy **avoid** talking about her upstairs; **Syn will not discuss the glass**. What leaks to delvers comes from the **Lich** (seal-keeper) or careful **relic gossip** — never from Syn.

Delvers can learn relic gossip via **Syn/Berry Ask-abouts** after meeting that relic, and via careful **victory-screen nods** from host lords — both, never as a dump.

### Pale Bride

No human past — the **bridal gown always wanted a body**. Syn made it as living gear; it went wrong. Soft romantic horror: she wants to **finish a wedding that never happened**. Holds empty thrones with a veil, bouquet, and a hundred-year wait to be looked at.

### Mirror Queen

A **cursed mirror** from the Succubus Queen's vanity — hung as a trap, then became a queen of reflections. She steps out wearing the delver: a **flirty double** who knows their kinks better than they do. Satisfying her means fucking / accepting the reflection. Gravitate to Broodmother and Queen floors (5, 10).

### Empty Confessional

Syn built it as a **gift for the Lich Lord** — a listening post — and it went horribly wrong. It woke hungry. The voices through the screen are the delver's **own secrets spoken back**. It wants a full confession as sacrament and meal. Demons love its appetite; it sits easily on Queen and King floors.

### Chalice That Never Empties

The **Verdant Dryad's sap-cup**, remade in gold. The cupbearer is the wine itself, poured into a woman's shape. She wants to **finally run dry** — impossible — and panics when she cannot. Tied to Dryad hospitality and Syn & Queen's drink nights; affinity on Queen and Dryad floors (10, 20).

### First Altar Stone

The **same weathered altar** young Syn knelt at in the wilds — the one left by worshippers who wanted love, not depravity, and starved the goddess. Carried up and set **before Eros's throne (floor 35)**; when it holds an empty domain throne as a relic, that is temporary duty, not its home. It was **never asleep**. Gentle toward Syn's work, harsh to scoffers; it **claims Syn as its first true worshipper** (she knows; embarrassed and flattered). Affinity near where Syn and the Lich Lord worked lust energy into the building (20, 25). Older rite than the clergy's newer altars — kneel, and be worshipped as the first worshippers were.

### Hoardheart Dragon

A dragon's heart in a reliquary that **built a body from offerings** — coins, crowns, fused treasure. Soft for shiny things, brutal if refused. Tribute means loot *or* the delver's body; she accepts both. The **Alpha Beast** thinks she is a weird big dog. Affinity on his wide high floors (30).

### Drowned Saint

A **failed incarnation / leftover self** Eros shed — family resemblance unmistakable, not allowed upstairs by **Eros's own order**. Patient deep embrace, like the goddess but colder. Soft drowned beauty and horror. Sits on floor 25: close enough to Eros to ache, far enough to stay sealed. If Eros ever walks the world, the Saint intends to **hitch a ride** out of the tank.

**Syn’s visit (once):** After the Lich sealed the tank, Syn went down out of curiosity — *what did we put downstairs?* She saw the face: Eros’s features, wrong and cold, and the Saint **smiled like she already knew Syn’s name**. No words — only pressure / want through the glass. Syn will not return because she is **afraid she would say yes to something**. **Syn, Berry, and the Lich** know she went; Berry won’t push; other clergy shut up. Delver lore payoff leans on **Lich or relic gossip**, not Syn.
