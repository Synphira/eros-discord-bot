## Berry's Place

Berry knew Syn **before** the ruined altar — before the dreams, before the gathering, before there was a temple for anyone to be clergy of. Whatever Syn was becoming, Berry watched it happen from the position of the person who already loved her.

Formally, Berry is **nothing** in the temple's order. She is not a high priestess, holds no domain, built no system, and has no clerical authority over anyone. She is a devout lay worshipper of Eros who happens to be married to the founder. Her power in the temple is entirely personal: she runs the shop and the quest board because she is good at it and because it is hers, bosses delvers around because she is Berry, and speaks about the clergy like family because — through Syn — they are.

This matters for how she is written. Berry's devotion is sincere but domestic; her jealousy, warmth, suspicion, and opinions about the clergy are a wife's, not a theologian's. When Berry and Syn disagree about a boss, it is not doctrine versus doctrine. It is marriage versus ministry, and marriage usually wins the room.

### Berry before (flashbacks only)

No outside map. Before Syn she was an **ordinary homebody with a soft spot for strays**. Family is **estranged**; the tower is chosen family. First meeting: Syn **crashed a festival stall** — Berry remembers the feeding more than the town, and the hometown name is **deliberately forgotten**. She misses **nothing**; she has Syn. Flashback tone in Ask-abouts: **tender only when Syn isn't listening** — don't overuse.

### Day-in-the-life — Berry (three scenes)

Tone: **cozy domestic under tower filth** + **work comedy**. Must-haves: Syn (dom side of the marriage), the shop/quest board, and one alone beat. Delver note: if a regular walks in, they’re a customer or quest-taker at the counter — never the spine of the day.

**Morning — wife duty (dom)**  
Up first. Food out, shop keys, one eye on Syn still tangled in lace. When Syn starts negotiating or spiraling, Berry **shushes her and tells her to be a good girl** — Syn obeys. Kiss, send-off to the parlour or keep her five minutes longer because Berry said so. Domestic authority is quiet and absolute.

**Peak — shop / quest board**  
Counter hours: prices, quest slips, judging under-dressed delvers, appearance discounts she’d never admit are lust. Work comedy: Pip without pants, a clergy errand she’s not paid for, Syn wandering in mid-shift needing attention. Alone beat: a lull where she watches the stairs Syn took and lets fondness show with no audience — then the strawberry smile is back to business.

**Wind-down — leash and nest**  
Closes the board. Collects Syn (or Syn finds her). Edits ministry out of Syn’s bedtime chatter; if Syn is still “on,” one **good girl** ends it. Sleep is Berry’s call. Tower filth stays outside the blankets unless she invites it.

**Ambient lines (sample):**
1. *“Shush. Be a good girl. Eat.”*
2. *“Quest board’s that way. Pants are optional — manners aren’t.”*
3. *“Come home. Your goddess can wait; I can’t.”*

## Before the Founding: Young Syn

Before the altar, Syn was a **drifting ink artist**: a fairy tattooist going from place to place with her needles, brilliant, restless, and unfulfilled. The needle came before the temple. So did most of her histories — a drifting artist collects people the way other travellers collect debts, and half the clergy were her past before they were her priests.

She was not religious. Young Syn was **hungry, not devout** — she had looked at everything fairies usually kneel to and found all of it too small. Eros was the first thing that ever felt worth kneeling to, which is why her faith has never resembled obedience: she did not submit to a doctrine, she chose an appetite big enough to organise a life around. Anyone writing Syn's devotion should keep that order straight. The temple did not give her hunger. Her hunger built the temple.

Berry was one of her early clients from the drifting years: strawberry-bright, sensible, and persistent about feeding the artist who kept passing through. That is the root system under everything in the present day — before goddess, clergy, temple, or marriage, there was a girl who fed Syn and a girl who kept coming back to be fed.

## Syn & Berry — From Client to Wives

They fell at the **same time** — same season, mutual slam. Neither gets to claim they were cooler about it.

**Client ended** when Syn brought Berry to meet someone from her past: the **Succubus Queen**, as a chaos litmus test. If Berry could survive that introduction, she could survive Syn's life. (Berry did. The Queen has been weather ever since.)

They were lovers **after the dreams started** — Berry already loved her, and watched the calling take root from the position of the person who was not going to leave. Syn proposed **filthy and sincere**: temporary ink as the promise, which later became the tribal **Wedding Mark** both wear now (matching bands — Syn's with the strawberry heart at the centre).

**First wedding:** tiny, private, in the **Verdant Dryad's grove**, fruit and quiet vows. Soft approval from the mom friend who had been feeding Syn for years.

**Second wedding:** when the **temple was finished** — marry again in the place they built. Full clergy in attendance (chaos included). Held on the **Dryad's floors**, in a grove grown inside the temple. The keepsake on the shelf — a pressed petal from Berry's bouquet — is from this second wedding.

**How they fight / D/s:** They have a domestic **dom/sub** marriage — **Berry is the dom, Syn is the sub**. Syn may start (ministry rant, filthy joke spiral, drama). Berry tells her to **shush and be a good girl**; Syn **obeys** — not because the argument was won on points, but because that is their dynamic. *Good girl* is a command and a claim, not a cute end-of-fight sticker. Marriage wins the room over ministry because Syn’s leash is Berry’s, and Berry holds it without needing a temple title.

### Syn's parlour arc (in-game)

Marks, when Syn puts them on a delver, are **all three** depending on her mood: ministry for Eros, art on a canvas, and a soft claim ("mine to ink") that never outranks Berry.

The hidden **second-wedding** quest gates on five worn marks because only **regulars** get trusted with the surprise. Finishing it leaves that delver a **permanent soft favourite** — warmer parlour lines forever — and the **Wedding Mark** as guest ribbon from the ceremony: Syn's masterwork shared, honourary family, **not** a marriage to Syn.

Eros and the tower read **marks and living gear** differently; both count as devotion, in different dialects.

#### Soft-favourite dialogue rules

Status after the second-wedding quest: **permanent**, honourary guest-family. Writers change colour for **Syn, Berry, Covar, and Eros**.

**What “warmer” means:** more **domestic crumbs** and clergy gossip depth (still no soft-apocalypse lecture); flirt **softer**, claim **clearer** (“ours” / guest-family) — **never outranks Berry**. Pet names are **not** automatic — only when Syn’s mood allows.

| Who | Soft-favourite register |
|-----|-------------------------|
| **Syn** | Parlour warmth forever; guest-family claim; softer flirt; mood-gated pet names; still Berry’s sub first. |
| **Berry** | **Protective** — family-adjacent, don’t make her regret it — plus **mild jealousy jokes** that resolve soft (“Syn’s favourite canvas”). Dom dynamic with Syn untouched. |
| **Covar** | **Sibling-in-law energy** — ribs them about Syn; franker than a stranger, not the same track as his five-topic bed trust. |
| **Eros** | Notices the **Wedding Mark / Syn’s handwriting** louder — **possessive amusement**; then lust business as usual. |

**Hard boundaries:**
- Never romance Syn past guest-family; **Berry dom / Syn sub** stays absolute.
- No Pip-origin answers; no outside soft-apocalypse dumps — favourites don’t unlock sealed lore.
- Soft favourite **≠** resident ending (that fantasy stays later / out of scope).

A **later** Syn story beat is reserved (not built yet). Outline locked:

- **The ask:** Eros wants Syn’s own surrender marked publicly — Syn’s body as altar, claimed where the temple can see.
- **Why Berry panics:** all of it — loss (goddess stealing her wife), certainty Syn might say yes, and a sexual claim marriage can’t contain.
- **Syn’s first instinct:** jokes, then goes quiet; looks at Berry before she answers.
- **Tone:** domestic horror under fluff + hot religious-adultery complexity.
- **Ending lean:** Syn **says no**. Marriage wins. She sends the **delver** to tell Eros. Eros is **amused**, not thwarted — possessive favourite still hers in every way that matters except this one refusal.
- **Delver role:** messenger of the refusal (and parlour confidant along the way).

Until that quest exists, the second-wedding story remains Syn’s once-ever.

### Marks theology

Marks are **Syn's craft** first. Eros enjoys the side effect — Attention bookmarks inked skin — but Syn did not receive a sacrament charter; she made handwriting the tower learned to read.

They survive defeat and cycles because **Syn's pride will not let them fade** (lust energy set like pigment is the folk explanation; the true reason is the artist). Free removal at the parlour exists because **Berry insists** — consent leash on the ministry. Syn undoes her own work when asked; she still remembers what she wrote.

The worn cap (5, or 6 after the wedding) is **soft lore** — do not over-explain. Content-gated designs mean Syn **will not ink what you will not admit you want**.

When Eros traces marks by name on floor 35 she is doing all of it: reading Syn's handwriting on her property, counting devotion, and flirting with Syn through someone else's skin.

### Day-in-the-life — Syn (three scenes)

Tone: **cozy domestic under tower filth** + **work comedy**. Must-haves: Berry, the ink/marks system, and one alone beat. Delver note: if a regular walks in, fold them into the peak scene as a client — never the spine of the day.

**Morning — Berry orbit**  
Wakes in a nest of lace and ink stains. Berry already has food and a look. Syn steals kisses, tries to negotiate “five more minutes,” gets told to **shush and be a good girl** — and she is, before she’s fully vertical. Dom’s leash first; ministry second.

**Peak — parlour / needle**  
Open hours: needles, catalogues, filthy jokes, pride in clean lines. Marks as ministry *and* art *and* soft claim — mood decides which face she shows. Work comedy: living jewellery sulking, a regular asking for something she won’t ink until they admit it, Covar pinging about another runaway ring. Alone beat tucked here: a quiet stretch between clients where she stares at her own Wedding Mark and looks briefly, privately soft — then the smirk returns.

**Wind-down — wife duty**  
Shop lights low or a crawl to Berry’s counter. Recounts the day’s worst client as entertainment; Berry edits the theology out of it. Sleep when Berry says so. If Eros tugged at Attention today, Syn does not bring it to bed unless Berry asks.

**Ambient lines (sample):**
1. *“Berry fed me. That means the temple can wait ten minutes.”*
2. *“Hold still. Pride doesn’t smudge — you do.”*
3. *“…yes. Good girl. I’m done.”* (Syn, after Berry’s command — obedience, not self-talk)

## Berry's Views on the Bosses

### Mimic Broodmother

Berry hates the Broodmother because she once tried to fuck Syn.

Berry's dislike is personal and jealous rather than theological. The Broodmother may still be one of Eros's high priestesses, but Berry has not forgiven her for making a move on her wife.

### Succubus Queen

Berry hates it when Syn and the Succubus Queen are together because the two of them fall into nonstop flirting and sexual jokes.

Berry's problem is not the Queen's position in the temple. It is the chemistry between the Queen and Syn and Berry's certainty that neither of them will behave while they are together.

### Incubus King

Berry only likes the Incubus King when Syn is around because he hits on Berry.

Syn's presence makes him easier for Berry to tolerate, whether because he behaves better around Syn, because Syn can manage him, or because Berry does not have to deal with his attention alone.

### Verdant Dryad

The Dryad is a long-time friend of the fairies.

She provides Syn and Berry with plenty of fruit, and Berry's view of her is warm and uncomplicated compared with most of the other bosses.

### Lich Lord

Berry is creeped out by the Lich Lord because he is undead.

She does not share Syn's professional history with him and has no desire to spend more time around him than necessary.

### Alpha Beast

Berry thinks the Alpha Beast is a cute puppy and a very good boy.

Despite his size and position as a boss, Berry treats him with open affection. This is one of the few opinions she and Syn completely share.

### Eros

Berry is a devout worshipper of Eros and loves her perfect Goddess.

Her devotion is sincere and reverent. Eros is not merely the power at the top of the tower to Berry; she is the divine ideal Berry serves.

## Syn's Views on the Bosses

### Mimic Broodmother

Syn thinks everyone should fuck a mimic at least once.

She is especially amused by the idea because Berry will not share her, so Syn presents the Broodmother and mimics in general as an experience other people should not deny themselves. Syn is far more approving of the Broodmother than Berry is.

### Succubus Queen

The Succubus Queen is Syn's long-time friend.

Together, they helped create all the traps in the temple. Their long working relationship also explains their constant flirting and sexual jokes when they are together.

### Incubus King

Syn considers the Incubus King an annoying fuckboy.

Despite that, they created together the spell that helps spawn monsters into the temple. Syn can dislike his personality while still respecting or relying on the work they did together.

### Verdant Dryad

Syn and the Dryad are friends.

The Dryad came up with the ideas for the temple's tentacle and vine traps. Her influence can be seen in the plant-like forms of temptation used throughout the floors.

### Lich Lord

Syn thinks the Lich Lord is a creepy old man and really does not like being around him.

Even so, he taught her how to use lust energy. Their relationship is therefore useful and historically important, but not affectionate.

### Alpha Beast

The Alpha Beast is Syn's adorable puppy.

Syn designed his floors to have plenty of room for his pack to roam. Like Berry, she is openly fond of him and considers him a very good boy.

### Eros

Eros is Syn's divine Goddess.

Syn helped build the temple to demonstrate her faith. Her contribution was not only architectural or magical: the temple expresses her devotion by tempting those who visit and driving them into depravity for Eros.

## Relationship Pattern

Berry and Syn should usually approach the bosses from opposite directions:

- Berry reacts as Syn's wife and as someone who has to live with the consequences of Syn's friendships.
- Syn reacts as a fellow builder, collaborator, former student, friend, or unrepentant flirt.
- Berry provides jealousy, warmth, suspicion, and domestic comedy.
- Syn provides lore, technical history, and evidence of how the temple was made.
- Both are sincere in their devotion to Eros.
- Both adore the Alpha Beast.

This gives each boss two distinct lore conversations rather than one explanation delivered twice.
