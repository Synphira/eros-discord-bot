# Lore — Endless Ruins of Sin

Status: living design document. These are initial ideas recorded on 2026-10-21 and are not all implemented yet.

## The Core Secret

The dungeon is not really a dungeon. It is a temple built to worship **Eros, Goddess of Lust**.

The six bosses below Eros and **Syn** are her high priests and priestesses. Together, they built the temple as an act of devotion. Its monsters, traps, events, transformations, curses, and repeated descents are intended to tempt visitors, break down their restraint, and drive them toward lust and depravity in Eros's name.

Eros herself is not one of the temple's builders in the same sense. She is the goddess the temple serves and the final presence waiting at its top.

Syn is also one of Eros's high priestesses and one of the temple's builders. Berry's exact religious position is still to be decided. She is Syn's wife, runs the shop and quest board, and is a devout worshipper of Eros.

## What the Temple's Design Means

The temple was not built merely to be explored. It was built to change the people who enter it.

Syn's particular act of faith was helping to create a place that draws visitors deeper into desire. The traps, monsters, hybrid forms, altars, curses, and other systems can therefore be understood as parts of the temple's ministry, even when the player does not yet know that.

The bosses are not simply obstacles placed before Eros. Each one helped shape the temple and continues to serve her in their own domain. Their floors, traps, monsters, and preferred forms of temptation should reflect their personalities and contributions.

Known contributions include:

- **Syn and the Succubus Queen** created the temple's traps together.
- **The Verdant Dryad** contributed the ideas behind the tentacle and vine traps.
- **Syn and the Incubus King** created the spell that helps spawn monsters throughout the temple.
- **The Lich Lord** taught Syn how to use lust energy.
- **Syn** designed the Alpha Beast's floors with plenty of room for his pack to roam.

These contributions can be revealed gradually through dialogue, boss encounters, room descriptions, and other lore fragments.

## The Temple Reveal

Players should not initially be told that the dungeon is a temple.

### Syn and Berry's language rule

Syn and Berry must never call the place a **dungeon**.

Before the temple reveal, they should keep its nature vague and use indirect language such as:

- "the tower"
- "this place"
- "these halls"
- "the floors"
- "up here" or "down there," where appropriate

They should not accidentally confirm that it is a temple before the reveal.

After the truth is revealed, Syn and Berry can openly call it **the temple**.

This restriction applies specifically to Syn and Berry. General narration, system text, other characters, and player-facing labels may continue to use words such as dungeon, ruins, or tower unless a later lore pass decides otherwise.

### Reveal timing

The exact reveal trigger has not been decided yet. Possible future candidates include defeating Eros, completing a story sequence, reaching a particular cycle, or following a dedicated sequence involving Syn, Berry, the bosses, or Eros's altars. This should be settled before the boss-conversation feature is written in full.

## Ask About the Bosses

**Talk to Berry** and **Talk to Syn** buttons on the quest board and at the parlour open a conversation screen for each fairy. Defeating or satisfying a boss unlocks that boss as a topic with both of them. The conversation menu currently holds 17 topics (10 general, 7 bosses); Discord select menus allow 25 options, so there is room to grow.

The same boss should produce different conversations depending on who is asked:

- **Berry** gives the domestic, emotional, and relationship-focused view. She is open about jealousy, affection, discomfort, and devotion.
- **Syn** gives more of the temple's history and explains how the boss contributed to building it, while also revealing her own flirtatious history and professional relationships.

The Alpha Beast is the exception to their disagreement: both Syn and Berry adore him and regard him as a very good boy.

The unlock is based on the bosses the player has defeated or satisfied (the `bosses_seen` record). Conversations can be repeated freely; whether later cycles add new variants still needs to be decided. General topics also unlock from experience — first monster defeated, first trap, first curse, first mark, first hybrid form, first altar offering, first completed cycle.

## Berry's Views on the Bosses

### Mimic Broodmother

Berry hates the Broodmother because she once tried to fuck Syn.

Berry's dislike is personal and jealous rather than theological. The Broodmother may still be one of Eros's high priestesses, but Berry has not forgiven her for making a move on her wife.

### Succubus Queen

Berry hates it when Syn and the Succubus Queen are together because the two of them fall into nonstop flirting and sexual jokes.

Berry's problem is not the Queen's position in the temple. It is the chemistry between the Queen and Syn and Berry's certainty that neither of them will behave while they are together.

### Incubus King

Berry only likes the Incubus King when Syn is around because he hits on Berry.

Syn's presence makes him easier for Berry to tolerate, whether because he behaves better around Syn, because Syn can manage him, or because Berry does not have to deal with his attention alone.

### Verdant Dryad

The Dryad is a long-time friend of the fairies.

She provides Syn and Berry with plenty of fruit, and Berry's view of her is warm and uncomplicated compared with most of the other bosses.

### Lich Lord

Berry is creeped out by the Lich Lord because he is undead.

She does not share Syn's professional history with him and has no desire to spend more time around him than necessary.

### Alpha Beast

Berry thinks the Alpha Beast is a cute puppy and a very good boy.

Despite his size and position as a boss, Berry treats him with open affection. This is one of the few opinions she and Syn completely share.

### Eros

Berry is a devout worshipper of Eros and loves her perfect Goddess.

Her devotion is sincere and reverent. Eros is not merely the power at the top of the tower to Berry; she is the divine ideal Berry serves.

## Syn's Views on the Bosses

### Mimic Broodmother

Syn thinks everyone should fuck a mimic at least once.

She is especially amused by the idea because Berry will not share her, so Syn presents the Broodmother and mimics in general as an experience other people should not deny themselves. Syn is far more approving of the Broodmother than Berry is.

### Succubus Queen

The Succubus Queen is Syn's long-time friend.

Together, they helped create all the traps in the temple. Their long working relationship also explains their constant flirting and sexual jokes when they are together.

### Incubus King

Syn considers the Incubus King an annoying fuckboy.

Despite that, they created together the spell that helps spawn monsters into the temple. Syn can dislike his personality while still respecting or relying on the work they did together.

### Verdant Dryad

Syn and the Dryad are friends.

The Dryad came up with the ideas for the temple's tentacle and vine traps. Her influence can be seen in the plant-like forms of temptation used throughout the floors.

### Lich Lord

Syn thinks the Lich Lord is a creepy old man and really does not like being around him.

Even so, he taught her how to use lust energy. Their relationship is therefore useful and historically important, but not affectionate.

### Alpha Beast

The Alpha Beast is Syn's adorable puppy.

Syn designed his floors to have plenty of room for his pack to roam. Like Berry, she is openly fond of him and considers him a very good boy.

### Eros

Eros is Syn's divine Goddess.

Syn helped build the temple to demonstrate her faith. Her contribution was not only architectural or magical: the temple expresses her devotion by tempting those who visit and driving them into depravity for Eros.

## Relationship Pattern

Berry and Syn should usually approach the bosses from opposite directions:

- Berry reacts as Syn's wife and as someone who has to live with the consequences of Syn's friendships.
- Syn reacts as a fellow builder, collaborator, former student, friend, or unrepentant flirt.
- Berry provides jealousy, warmth, suspicion, and domestic comedy.
- Syn provides lore, technical history, and evidence of how the temple was made.
- Both are sincere in their devotion to Eros.
- Both adore the Alpha Beast.

This gives each boss two distinct lore conversations rather than one explanation delivered twice.
